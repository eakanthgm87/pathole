How it works

Frames are sent to the server one at a time and the next is only grabbed once the previous result lands, so the stream paces itself to whatever the model can sustain.

Nothing is stored unless you press Save this frame. Saved captures become ordinary reports and need a GPS fix.

Getting good results

  • Hold the camera at a shallow angle, not straight down
  • Daylight helps a lot; the model is weak at dusk
  • Water-filled holes are the hardest case
  • On a phone, use the rear camera
